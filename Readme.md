HomeBuy is an AI-powered home shopping assistant designed to automate the purchase of household goods and essential items.

The app allows users to schedule recurring purchases (weekly, monthly, quarterly, etc.), and HomeBuy automatically orders and delivers those items directly to their doorstep.

Instead of repeatedly shopping for everyday household supplies, users simply configure their preferences once and let the system handle the rest.
